Welcome to the Academy

The Core Loop

Click the main cat to earn coins. Spend coins in the Store to buy generators that earn coins passively. Head to Upgrades to multiply your clicking power and generation rate.

Battle Arena

In the Battle Arena, your Click Power translates directly to Active Damage, and your Passive Income becomes Damage Per Second. Defeating enemies drops huge proportional coin rewards.

Ascension & Infinite Scaling

Because Feline Ascendancy utilizes BigInt architecture, numbers will scale infinitely. Once you reach 1 Trillion lifetime coins, you can Ascend. Ascension wipes your current progress but grants Meta Currency via an exponential equation, permanently boosting all future loops.
